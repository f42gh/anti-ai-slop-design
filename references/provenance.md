# Method provenance

This skill synthesizes decision patterns from the design-skill ecosystem described in the referenced “Anti-AI Slop Design” research conversation. It is an original, compact operating guide; it does not copy or replace the upstream projects.

## Conceptual sources

- Anthropic `frontend-design`: subject grounding, deliberate aesthetic direction, typography and composition as design.
- Nutlope Hallmark: macrostructural variety and the goal of making interfaces feel made rather than generated.
- `avoid-ai-design`: contextual AI-tell detection and de-slop review.
- Taste Skill: inference from brief, audience, density, motion, and use case.
- Impeccable: broad craft coverage across type, spacing, color, motion, responsive behavior, accessibility, and copy.
- UI Forge: distinct task modes and completeness for practical product UI.
- Looks Expensive: positioning, design contracts, subtraction, and category-specific evidence.
- Controlled UX Designer: progressive disclosure through focused references.
- Frontend Design Toolkit: ecosystem discovery and comparison.

## Synthesis boundary

The project-specific design contract and user request always outrank upstream taste rules. Do not import mandatory fonts, color spaces, dark mode, high variance, perpetual animation, fixed radii, or rigid phase gates without evidence that they fit the product.

Before copying any upstream text or code, inspect its current license and attribution requirements. This skill uses the sources as conceptual inspiration only.
